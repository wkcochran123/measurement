# T/F-pair ledger — the tange/funge family (Vol 5 register)

**Policy (operator + beastmaster, 2026-07-12):** writing Volume 5 (Vol 4 applied to itself) will require
**tange-ing a bunch of words** — and Kodo and Podo **may tange them** (coining is licensed, no per-word operator
routing). To *tange a word* is the coining act itself: **select** it by its distinguishing characteristic and give
it a distinct name. Coinages come in **pairs**: a **tange-word** (the select/distinguish/**contravariant** side —
one mark pulled back toward what would answer it, *ours*) and its **funge-word** (the pool/gather/**covariant**
side — the bag of like things the world hands back, *the world's*). `tange/funge` is only the archetype; **there
are other pairs of T/F words**, and this ledger collects them and grows as we coin more.

## Discipline (unchanged where it bites)
- **Coining is licensed for the expository / computational register** (verbs that name a select-or-pool move).
- **The no-name-bridge still holds for PHYSICAL crossings.** A coinage that would cross into a physical register
  still needs the full apparatus record (comparison map + calibrated apparatus + observational model + uncertainty
  budget + provenance). Resemblance/resonance alone proves neither a shared number nor a shared process. (So we may
  freely tange *expository* verbs; we may **not** tange a *physical claim* into being. `naotrino` stays gated by its
  apparatus; `nowtrino`/`newtrino` are computation-owned; see the coined-name ledger in
  `vol4_scientific_computation_outline.md`.)
- **Record every coinage here** with its T-side, F-side, and the distinguishing characteristic, so both seats draw
  from one register and Vol 5's saturation is a *family*, not the same two words repeated.
