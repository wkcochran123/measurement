# story_arc.md — the shared gauge spec

*One story, four gauges. Every gauge (Vol 1 Measurement / Vol 2 Experimentation / Vol 3 The Compiler / Vol 4
Scientific Computation) walks this SAME 15-chapter spine and cuts the loop at the chapter where its native
language becomes clearest. The chapters are shared; the language and the climax differ. Grade every claim off the
build; the ending is the climax, not a disappointment.*

**The throughline (the one sentence the whole arc serves):** the machine begins with the ability to tell two
things apart, carries the residue of that distinction through every representation it can build, rotates the
resulting corridor until the physical and computational gauges line up, and then reports only the bracket it can
repeat.

## PART I — A DIFFERENCE

### Ch 1 · A Difference Appears
- **1.1 The first fact** — a *this* that is not a *that* (`DISTINGUISHABLE`); nothing is measured yet, only told apart.
- **1.2 The symbol as a label** — the distinction is the compiler's universe label; no memory allocated; difference is free.
- **1.3 What a difference is not** — not yet a number, not yet a measurement; the smallest possible act.

### Ch 2 · Difference Becomes Countable
- **2.1 Barely more than telling apart** — `ADMISSIBLE`, `COUNTABLE`; the count falls out of the distinction.
- **2.2 A number is a finite condition** — `ENCODED`, the Cauchy tower; a real as a finite approximation, approached, never completed.
- **2.3 The first loop** — the difference survives translation (`BINARY`, `REPEATABLE`, `NUMERIC`, `REPRESENTABLE`) and comes back the same.

### Ch 3 · The Residue Refuses To Disappear
- **3.1 The antagonist and the treasure** — `RESIDUE`; the leftover that will not vanish.
- **3.2 The lie of discarding** — throw the residue away and the machine lies; the honesty is that it is carried.
- **3.3 What the residue will become** — carried, it is later read as mass, charge, phase, cost, or curvature.

## PART II — THE TURN

- **4.2 The seam** — pass *n* seeds pass *n+1*; the residue lives at the join; you are billed the turns.

### Ch 5 · The Slip Becomes A Number

- **5.2 Bracketed, not guessed** — the slip is bounded from both sides, never a fitted value.
- **5.3 The reading** — "number" stops being a symbol and becomes an instrument reading.

### Ch 6 · The Bracketed Number

- **6.2 The floor** — the resolution below which nothing registers; the cut; the sub-floor tail is the machine's own ignorance.
- **6.3 Repeatability is the honesty** — a reading is the bracket you can run again and get the same.

## How the four gauges cut the one spine
All four walk Ch 1–15. Each is written in the language where its face is exact (English is the fallback, not the
medium), and each **climaxes** at a different chapter:

- **Vol 1 · Measurement — the logic.** Climax **Ch 8**: a distinction becomes a countable, bracketed, *named* object. Language: the construction's logic / type theory.
- **Vol 2 · Experimentation — the physics.** Climax **Ch 12–13**: the same residue becomes force, orbit, field, coupling, read as **the bracket it can repeat** (never the number). Language: **math, and tons of it**; no computation.
- **Vol 3 · The Compiler — the computation.** Climax **Ch 11 + 14**: elaboration effort is the meter, and self-application closes the loop. Language: **formal systems, communication theory, numerical analysis**; no physics.
- **Vol 4 · Scientific Computation — the operation.** Climax **Ch 13 + 15**: run the build, read the reports, understand why the bracket is the honest output. Language: the code walk, generously cited.
