Section title: One Clock, Two Proofs

Beat:
One clock, two proofs: decidability and elapsed cost become two readings of
the same proof burden.

Writer fragments:
- Fragment A: establish the problem. Chapter 02 ended with a unique positive
  invariant; Chapter 03 asks how that invariant is grounded by a concrete
  operator without changing the grammar.
- Fragment B: explain one clock. A clock is the bound under which a burden is
  read, not an external river of time.
- Fragment C: explain two readings. Decidability reads whether a burden
  resolves inside the permitted grammar; elapsed cost reads how much clock is
  spent to resolve it.
- Fragment D: merge. The same burden must be read both as answerability and
  as cost; examples may mention latency, but only as illustration.

Acceptance criteria:
- Use "burden" and "clock" as grammatical terms.
- Do not make the example the spine.
- End by forcing the need for a concrete chain with a boundary.
