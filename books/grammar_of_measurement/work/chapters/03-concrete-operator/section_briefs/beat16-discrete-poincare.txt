Beat:
Discrete Poincare: a boundary-anchored chain forces zero-energy nodes to
zero.

Writer fragments:

- Fragment B: explain zero energy. If every neighboring difference vanishes,

- Fragment C: explain the anchor. The boundary identifies one node and forbids
  the null drift that the interior form cannot read.
- Fragment D: conclude that zero energy now forces every node to zero; this is
  the finite Poincare reading.
