Section-manager brief: Beat 28
================================

Working title: The Extensional Ledger

Beat:
- Extensional Cohen ledger: finite conditions become extensional and
  countable.

Required section result:
- Explain how Chapter 04's finite Cohen conditions lift from presentation
  to extensional reading.
- A condition is identified by the atoms, supports, bounds, and decisions
  it carries, not by the order or name under which it was written.
- The quotient must preserve finite support and recoverability.
- Countability must survive the quotient: finite atoms have finite indices,
  finite lists of indexed atoms can be enumerated, and extensional
  identification removes duplicates without adding new continuum points.

Writer-sized fragments:
1. Inherit the finite Cohen ledger from Chapter 04 and state why
   presentation is too cheap.
2. Define extensional equality in prose: same carried obligations, same
   overlaps, same decisions within the bound.
3. Explain merge and extension under extensional reading.
4. Explain countability of the extensional ledger.
5. Close by handing the countable ledger to the commutator residue.

Examples allowed:
- Cantor-Godel-Cohen recoverability: completion may not add distinctions
  unrecoverable by refinement.
- Precision: interpolated values are consequences of admissible
  completion, not new measurements.
