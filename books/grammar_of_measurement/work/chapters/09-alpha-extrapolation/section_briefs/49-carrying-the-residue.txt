Section manager brief: beat 49 -- Carrying the residue
======================================================

Beat:
Repeated trials carry seed and residue without restarting.

Section task:
Explain how a bounded trial advances by carrying state. Each pass keeps the
seed, the selected aperture face, the carried residue, and the accumulated
variance. A new trial extends the ledger; it does not wipe the previous trial
clean. The grammar must distinguish a fresh question from a repeated question
whose residue has been carried forward.

Required manuscript moves:
- A trial permits another sample only by appending to the existing ledger.
- The seed is a carried selector, not an arbitrary restart.
- The residue is both memory and obligation: it records what the prior pass did
  not absorb and supplies the starting remainder for the next pass.
- The bound prevents unspent residue from being hidden in an infinite future.

Useful illustrations:
- Repeated measurements under a stable alphabet.
- Laser pulse tracking as a growing ledger of returned constraints.
- Finite precision saturation as a reason a bound is part of the reading.
