Section manager brief: beat 50 -- The three rungs of alpha
==========================================================

Beat:
Aperture faces map to charge, mass, and value.

Section task:
Explain the aperture as one bounded bracket read through three rungs. The same
aperture is not three different worlds; it is one bracket whose moments are read
as charge, mass, or value. The grammar cycles charge -> mass -> value -> charge
again, so a fourth reading forces a quotient with the first.

Required manuscript moves:
- The aperture is a finite bracket: lower, upper, and width.
- Charge reads loop count/orientation.
- Mass reads second-difference strain.
- Value reads the surviving normalized result that can be compared across
  trials.
- The three rungs force a ceiling on what one finite aperture can resolve.

Useful illustrations:
- Three-output finite alphabet examples.
- Precision vs accuracy: interpolated values are consequences, not new facts.
