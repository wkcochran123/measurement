Section manager brief: beat 57 -- The replicating portfolio for the neutrino
============================================================================

Beat:
Exact surface observables stand in for the unobservable interior.

Section task:
Explain the neutrino as a replicating portfolio. When an unobservable
exclusion interior and an ordinary Cooper-pair interior cast the same mass,
charge, spin-gravity residue, and alpha at the surface, the grammar lets the
surface portfolio stand in for the hidden interior. The portfolio replicates
surface payoffs; it does not open the horizon.

Required manuscript moves:
- A portfolio is exact only with respect to exported observables.
- The grammar identifies surface payoffs, not unseen inner causes.
- The neutrino label names the exclusion residue carried by that exact surface
  replication.
- If a proposed portfolio changes any exported observable, it fails.

Useful illustrations:
- Financial replication as an analogy: match the boundary payoffs, not the
  internal path.
- Neutrino transport as a minimal curvature/exclusion messenger.
