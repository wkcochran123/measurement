Section manager brief: beat 55 -- The collapse of the Cooper pair horizon
=========================================================================

Beat:
Ordinary pair interior and exclusion interior become indistinguishable.

Section task:
Explain how the horizon masks two different interior stories: an ordinary
spin-balanced Cooper-pair interior and a neutrino-exclusion interior. They are
not declared the same as interior mechanisms. They become the same read only
after the horizon quotients interiors by exact surface agreement.

Required manuscript moves:
- Preserve the difference between hidden interiors before the quotient.
- State that the horizon forbids inspection of which interior story produced
  the surface.
- The grammar identifies the interiors only because every exported surface
  observable agrees.
- The collapse is a surface collapse, not a global identity of all internal
  descriptions.

Useful illustrations:
- Superconducting pair as strain-free paired transport.
- Meissner exclusion as a region forbidding antisymmetric curvature inside.
