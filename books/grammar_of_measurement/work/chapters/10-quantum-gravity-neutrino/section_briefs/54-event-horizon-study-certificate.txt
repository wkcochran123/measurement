Section manager brief: beat 54 -- The event horizon study certificate
=====================================================================

Beat:
The approach to the horizon is bounded by cost.

Section task:
Turn the lightlike direction into a certificate. A sequence of timelike radii
approaches the horizon from above. Each reading carries a gap, a velocity
reading, a relativistic coefficient, and a cost. The certificate says that the
approach is lawful only as a bounded study, not as an unbounded crossing.

Required manuscript moves:
- The grammar permits an approach sequence and forbids pretending that the
  horizon has been crossed.
- Cost is part of the certificate: finer approach spends more burden.
- The horizon is a latency/export boundary: interior refinements may continue,
  but finite mergeability with the exterior fails at the limit.
- The study reads timelike approach to a lightlike boundary.

Useful illustrations:
- Event Horizon and Schwarzschild descriptions: export of distinguishable
  refinements becomes unbounded.
- A nondecreasing cost record as the approach is refined.
