Section manager brief: beat 59 -- Flavor as the exclusion label
================================================================

Beat:
Flavor is read as the difference between seeded horizon alpha and charge-only
alpha.

Section task:
Explain that flavor is the allowed horizon label. The reader-seeded horizon
alpha may differ from charge-only alpha because the hidden exclusion channel
has changed the surface receipt. The difference is not color and not a visible
interior mechanism; it is the flavor residue of the exclusion label.

Required manuscript moves:
- Read flavor as a kind label preserved by surface correction.
- The flavor residue is the horizon alpha minus charge-only alpha where that
  difference is positive in the surface ledger.
- Subtracting the flavor correction restores the charge-only alpha without
  opening the horizon.
- The correction must preserve the same-surface fact.

Useful illustrations:
- Flavor as the label of a permitted exclusion channel.
- Neutrino as the messenger of missing correlants or curvature correction.
