Section-manager brief: Beat 2, Counting by wholes and parts
============================================================

Target section file:
- sections/02-wholes-and-parts.tex

Beat:
- Same-parity readings count by wholes, while different-parity readings count by
  parts.

Writer-sized fragments:
- Move from distinction to count without assuming completed arithmetic.
- Explain same-parity as a whole-preserving reading and different-parity as a
  part-making reading.
- Show how count is already two-sided: pooling and selecting, preservation and
  fracture.

Constraints:
- Keep the logic grammatical, not computational.
- Examples may invoke ledgers, marks, repeated readings, or instrument records.
