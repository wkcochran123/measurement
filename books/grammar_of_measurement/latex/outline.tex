\chapter*{Outline of the Device}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Outline of the Device}

This book is organized as a reading manual for a finite measuring
instrument. The instrument does not ask the reader to believe a number. It
asks the reader to submit a claim, run a repeatable check, and read the
residual that remains above or below the floor.

The outline below is the order of the device. Each chapter is a gauge on the
same reading. Later passes through the manuscript should revise one section at
a time against this order.

\section*{Part I: The Floor}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The Verifier and the Sacred Floor.}
The book opens on the public surface of the instrument: a claim enters, the
machine compares it with the calibrated blank, and the result returns as
verified or outgrown. The sacred floor is not a mood or a tradition. It is the
cost of recognizing the clean identity before any interesting claim is read.

\item \textbf{Counting to Three.}
The first three gates are distinguishable, admissible, and countable. They are
the grammar's minimum spatial discipline. One distinction gives a reading, two
give an orientation, and three give a frame. Closure under the naturals is the
rule that lets the frame refine without pretending to own a continuum.

\item \textbf{The Needle.}
The second blank is
\[
.999 = 1 .
\]
This book reads it as a finite counted act. Three counted dimensions certify
the present box
\[
137.035999 \leq \alpha^{-1} \leq 137.036 .
\]
Sharper claims require another counted dimension and another report.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part II: The Gauge}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{3}
\item \textbf{The Polar Frame and the Local Lorentz Read.}
The three counted directions are written as a local polar frame and then read
through a local orthonormal frame. The signed norm is checked there, not in a
global polar fantasy. The radial, polar, and azimuthal legs become the three
places where the ledger can report a residue.

\item \textbf{The Three Radiation Channels.}
The radial leg is read as the alpha-particle channel, the polar tangent leg as
the beta-particle channel, and the azimuthal leg as the gamma-particle channel.
These names are channel labels. The alpha-particle channel is not the
fine-structure constant. The ledger must keep that distinction alive.

\item \textbf{Residue, Antimatter, and Conservation.}
Every reading has a residue. Above the floor the residue is claimable; below
the floor it is cut into antimatter, meaning unclaimable remainder. The ledger
balances only when output, residue, and the discarded tail have not hidden a
directional artifact.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part III: The Search}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{6}
\item \textbf{The Cost-Sorted Universe.}
The search walks a finite grammar of ground readings sorted by cost. The
rank, the length of the derivation, and the effort of the read are three ways
of seeing the same ladder once the bridge is closed. The search is honest only
because the floor and the finite pigeonhole stop it from pretending infinity is
free.

\item \textbf{The Fold and the Loop.}
The fold probe relabels a number into the corridor and reads it back. The open
path is flat. The closed loop exposes the remainder. Replacing a bare
identification by a division needle turns the residue into the quantity the
instrument can price.

\item \textbf{Acceleration, Strain, and Curvature.}
The accelerated frame reads a second difference. That strain is the finite
curvature the instrument can see. It is a volume-changing reading, not a
shape-preserving one, so the book names the capability fence clearly: the
device reads the traced curvature and is blind to the trace-free shape.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part IV: The Report}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{9}
\item \textbf{Running and Reading the Instrument.}
The final chapter is the user's protocol. Pre-register the claim, run the
verifier, read the residual, classify the landing as full or structural, and
refuse any sharper claim than the counted box licenses. The book ends where
the next experiment begins: count another dimension, then run again.
\end{enumerate}
