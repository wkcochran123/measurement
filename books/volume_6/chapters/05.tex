An honest instrument keeps two columns, and a book about one closes by balancing
them. In the first column is what the volume read; in the second, what it still
owes. The size of the debt does not diminish the reading --- it guarantees it,
because a book that owed nothing would be a book that claimed everything, and this
series has been an argument against exactly that since its first page. This
chapter sets the read beside the owed, and files, in a register of its own, the
conjectures the volume was pressed toward and declined.

What the volume read is one face, built in three readings. It read the fourth
description of the model as a \emph{coarsening} of the fine argument --- an exact
re-count at a coarser floor, no approximation in it, defining fewer distinctions
but losing nothing to averaging. It read, in that re-count, a \emph{strain} and
the \emph{taper} that damps it: the argument's resistance to being pinned, made
visible as a ringing, and the smooth fall-off that carries the ring down --- both
of them shapes the device lives by, exhibited as shapes and never as theorems.
And it read one thing that \emph{is} a theorem: the emergent behavior of the face,
the residue that survives the coarsening and the damping and returns unchanged
under a fold proved to close at three and no fewer. Three readings, one face: a
coarsening, a damped strain, and a proved invariant.

And it read a number, once the face was built, and the honest account of that
number is the load-bearing entry of this whole chapter. The deep display near
$137.011$ is read off the \emph{mediant} --- the bisection geometry the earlier
volumes built. The crossing there is a clean quadratic surd, $\sqrt{18/5} =
\sqrt{90}/5$, formed from two of the device's own quantities and no others: the
measured displacement, eighteen, and the target, five. Its continued fraction is
eventually periodic, as a quadratic irrational's must be, and its convergents ---
computed by exact division, not by any crawl --- bracket the crossing from
alternate sides. The count to three sets the bracket at $\left[\,\tfrac{17}{9},\,
2\,\right]$ in displacement, which reads as the inverse-coupling walls near
$129.6$ and $137.7$; and the deeper convergents settle, past the count of three,
toward the deep display near $137.011$. No grid, no seed, no external number: the
eighteen and the five are the device's own, and the number is what the mediant
converges to. That is how the number is read, and it is the only how the book
asserts.

One subtlety in that reading must be handled exactly, because it is the finest
point where the lab-gap could be misheard. The early, loose bracket ---
$\left[\,129.6,\, 137.7\,\right]$ --- happens to \emph{span} the laboratory's
measured value near $137.036$. It would be the whole series' error, made at its
most tempting, to hear ``the bracket contains the world's value'' as ``the device
reaches the world's value.'' It does not. The spanning is a property of the loose
early wall, the coarse two-to-one convergent, and nothing more; the device does
not land in the loose bracket, it \emph{converges past it} to its own display near
$137.011$, and the machine proves of itself that it cannot derive past that value
of its own toward the laboratory's. The world's $137.036$ is named here once, as
the world's, and it stays where every volume has kept it: not reached, not
approached, not bridged, even though a loose early bracket, by the arithmetic of
its coarsest wall, encloses it. The device lands on its own number; the world's
is outside, and the enclosure is a fact about a wide bracket, not a step across
the gap.

What the volume owes is shorter to state and no less honestly owed. The fourth
face was built as a \emph{description}; the deeper object --- a machine-checked
model of the flow itself, the thing that would replace the device's own
placeholder for this face with a carried, checked construction --- was not built
here, and is not claimed built. It is a debt of the device's own workshop, to be
paid where such things are paid, by building and checking, and it is filed owed.
The debt is discharged to the exact extent the volume built the description, and
the remainder is named, not disguised.

\section*{The conjecture register}

And here, in a register of its own, are the conjectures the volume was pressed
toward and did not cross into --- named, so the fences are not mistaken for the
things they hold back, and so no reader takes a declined conjecture for a result.

The first is the \emph{difference construction}: hold the two theories as tapes,
subtract, and take the residue --- the wager being that the residue is small, and
is the model's number. It is filed here as a \emph{bet}, and filed strictly apart
from the mediant. The number the volume reads is read by the mediant, established
above; the claim that a difference of the two frames would reach the same residue
is a conjecture the device has not built and this book does not assert. The two
must never be conflated: one is how the number is read, the other is a wager that
another route would reach it. The wager is recorded as a wager.

The second is the \emph{gross structure constant} --- a coined name for that
wager's residue, the overall counterpart to the fine structure constant the fifth
volume read. The name is honest as a name and dishonest as a claim: it labels the
conjecture just filed, not a value the device has read, and coining it turns a bet
into a named bet and nothing further.

The third is the \emph{tesseract labeling}: the state before a step read as the
one theory, the state after as the other, the step between as the fluid face. Its
real core is granted and cited: the model's first and second variation fall,
provably, into the same box, indistinguishable at the machine's floor, the
residue between them zero there. But the labeling over that core is a picture, and
the tell is the sign convention --- the assignment runs the other way just as
well by a change of sign, and a physical identity does not flip with a convention
while a label does. And the counts are held apart: the residue between the
variations is zero at the floor; the displacement itself is eighteen; a count that
would return zero over the two theories' own constructions is a build not run, and
none of the three is written as the crossing's confirmation. The eighteen, here,
is the one coupling read along one of its three paths, not a fresh independent
quantity; the book does not multiply its eighteens.

Set the columns side by side and the shape of the volume stands clear. It built
the fourth face as a description --- a coarsening, a damped strain, a proved
invariant --- and read the model's own number off the mediant, holding the
world's value outside even where a loose bracket encloses it. It did not build the
deeper model of the flow, and says so. And it declined, by name, every conjecture
that would have turned the shared wall into a crossing. What remains is a short
list of honest debts and a register of declined conjectures, and both are written
in the open. The last chapter tabulates the fences, as a series whose spine is its
boundaries should end.
