## The thesis (one breath — unchanged, now proven end to end)
There is **one** 35-step self-compiling program, and **what it computes depends on the carrier you run it
over**:

> over `Value` → it builds itself (the apparatus);
> over `Prop` (its own truth) → it reads itself and collapses (`true = false`);
> walked back down, traced, executed, and counted → it *is* the particle: the charge, the mass, the phase,
> and the one sameness that identifies them.

**Matter and code are the two outputs of one self-compilation.** What v1 could only sketch — the walk back
down — is now machinery: the descent returns, the return is traced, the trace is executed, and the
execution recovers a number in a bracket. The loop is closed in code.

## Why this shape (and what changed from v1)
Still chopped by passes = carriers = outputs. But the sketch/proven split that organized v1 has inverted:
Movement III no longer apologizes, and Movement IV's ledger ends not on "is it provable?" but on a short,
named list of what remains. **One new movement**: the machine's own laws — the grammar the construction
forced on its builders — earned a place when the poison, the seams, the needle, and the quoted Prop turned
out to be discoveries, not conventions. Five movements.

# MOVEMENT III — THE RETURN (the descent, the trace, the test, the number)
*The rework's center. v1's "Emission" was one movement of sketch; v2's "Return" is the same territory as
machinery, expanded to carry what was actually built. Every section proven unless marked.*

- **III.1 — The Backward Pass.** The descent: every rung of the tower run in reverse, each capability
  rebuilt from what came back rather than what went out. Ends at the needle close: the fact that returns is
  not a copy of the seed — it is SAME, "true and the output are the same truth," decided once, by the one
  sanctioned quotient-soundness. The direction of variation exists, is handled, and is never taken.
  *Proven (was v1's central sorry).*
- **III.2 — The Seam.** Pass n closes; pass n+1 opens on its residue. The seam grammar as the machine's
  own etiquette: world-readings tighten, ideologies re-mint, capabilities reset to identity, oaths repeat
  verbatim, new grounds re-prove occupancy. Iteration counted: the charge IS the loop count. *Proven.*
- **III.3 — The Trace.** The second tape: one cell per class, the descent writing what-was-built against
  what-the-ground-supplied — thirty-five cells and the anchor that seeds them, seam to lower bound, the
  bottom cell the origin fact traced directly. The tape only appends; the first mark is the caller's
  privilege. *Proven.*
- **III.4 — The Slip Test.** The trace executed back up. The register — the value at its current level,
  and the level itself — is the executor's state, carried in neither tape. The verdict is tape-borne: the
  tape's own order already encodes slipped-or-not (first-fact agreement, second-fact disagreement); the
  execution writes its record onto the trace it executes. **And the finding the audit handed us, audited
  not extrapolated: the entire execution layer is axiom-free** — every executor rung, the seam's exit, and
  the read-time decide depend on no axioms at all (exhaustively probed). The climb spends nothing — not
  even extensionality; the axioms enter only where the tower's classes do. *Proven; the axiom-free executor
  stated as measured.*
- **III.5 — The Three Trips.** The ladder walked three times, graded by force: not enough to move (the
  slip test says no, the level stays put); enough to start moving (the slip; one revolution counted);
  enough to recover mass (the reading climbs to the first constructor with a strain slot, and the strain
  read there IS the mass). Why three: mass is a second difference — null, threshold, response. **The
  equivalence principle as a theorem: the response and the strain are one field, two reads — mass
  recovered from the slot, never injected.** The number lands in its bracket: charge the lower bound,
  curvature the upper, the register's ride between. *Proven; the recovery closes by definitional
  reflexivity — the construction flat enough that the theorem is a single "it is so."*
- **III.6 — The Four Faces.** One number, four faces, three costs: two projections (free — charge and mass
  are already in the bracket), one decision (the phase, which asks the machine to decide a carried fact),
  and one identification (the sameness — not a reader but a theorem, the only face allowed to touch the
  needle). What a face costs is what knowing it costs. The butterfly interpretation of the charge face
  (the popcount currency). The concrete driver where every face reduces to a literal. *Proven except the
  agreement theorem [OPERATOR-GATED at drafting time — status to be updated].*
- **III.7 — The Two Outputs.** The thesis stated whole, now with the loop closed: one program; `Value` →
  apparatus, `Prop` → collapse, the return → the particle with its four faces. The electron algebra and
  the Cooper pair (the commutator at the value level; the Hamming meter; distance 0 the electron, distance
  1 the pair) — carried over from v1's discharge-design, still the reach toward superconductivity.
  *Interpretation on proven parts.*

# MOVEMENT IV — THE LAWS (what the machine taught its builders)
*New. The six laws + the corollary were not conventions we chose but walls we hit; each is stated with the
failure that taught it. This movement is the compiler book's own experimental physics: the elaborator as
the apparatus, the build as the measurement.*

- **IV.1 — The Poison and the Corollary.** Frozen instances; local binders that shadow; and the load-tested
  line: a projection through a tower-parameterized type re-synthesizes what the nearest binder wins —
  cross-chain reference must travel as plain data. Twenty cures and eleven predicted cleans — thirty-one
  calls, zero misses. The elaborator's own physics.
- **IV.2 — The Needle and the Seam Rules.** One truth told once; seams quote and never evaluate — and the
  machine enforced the second before we asked: a bare proposition slot *cannot* demand a decision, so the
  slip proposition rides the tape undecided and is decided only at read. Law discovered in the wild.
- **IV.3 — The Tapes and the Certificates.** The tape only appends; two tapes, never conflated — and the
  trace that carries its own execution as the discipline's reward. Certificates by exhaustion: every arm
  read, every count honest (the nine arms; the eleven leaves; the position-six strain).
- **IV.4 — The Measurement That Refused to Be Forced.** The stuck carrier: no instance search can invent a
  completed descent, so *which carrier emerges* is genuinely the use site's measurement — the machine
  declining to answer a question that only running it can answer. The device's closing thought, and the
  book's: a self-compiler can tell you what it is; only a run can tell you what it measured.
