## 1. The principle — two bands, one signal

The book already teaches sampling, the Nyquist limit, the Fourier pair, the Dirichlet kernel, filtering
(Ch07). The double read **uses the book's own instrument on the book.** The text is a single signal
carrying two bands:

- **the physics band** — the effects, the math, the instrument; **passed on the first read.**
- **the structure band** — orientation-is-yours / the turn that returns signed / the cruel laws and the
  one room they leave; **rejected on the first read, passed on the second.**

The second band is *always in the signal.* The second reading is the reader applying a new passband — the
**filter** the bookends handed them (the preface NAMED free will; the coda DELIVERED it: the orientation
is yours, the spinor, Q.E.D.). "There was more in the text" is literal: **nothing is added on re-read; the
filter stops rejecting what was always there.** (Read aloud once at the coda or in the preface, never as
instruction — the book's filter theme makes the metaphor honest, not cute.)

**The two bands are a covariant/contravariant pair** — the braid's two strands made into two readings. The
physics band is **covariant**: it runs *with* the construction (forward, the build going up) and is frame-
invariant — the count is the world's, unchanged under every relabeling. The structure band is
**contravariant**: it runs *against* (backward, the read-back, the compiled tower coming down) and transforms
*with* the reader's frame — the orientation is the reader's, and one turn flips its sign (the spinor). The
two readings pair the way a vector and a covector contract to a scalar: the covariant physics and the
contravariant structure contract to the book's meaning. **A passage "matches the intent of contravariant
readings" exactly when it can be read both ways — forward as physics, backward as structure.** (This is also
why Vol 3 tells the story from the funge, *backwards*: it is the contravariant reading made primary.)

**Grounded in the code (Episode1, confirmed turn-2 §1).** This pairing is not borrowed onto the book — it is
the construction's own routing. The code routes on **fact-parity**: same-truth orders **covariantly**
(counting by wholes = the **funge**); different-truth orders **contravariantly** (the n-th part = the
**tange**). So *covariant = funge = the count (the world's, invariant); contravariant = tange = the labels /
the selection (ours, frame-dependent).* The world/bookkeeping split and the residue base (a quotient that
pairs **without electing a representative** — choice-free) are where the contravariant band actually lives.
**Fidelity rule (a real catch, turn-2 §1):** the *first mark* is NOT a fork — its asymmetry is fixed by the
type system (a decidable inequality), no unchosen choice there. Do not seed the fork at the first
distinction; seed it in the labels and the residue. The code is ground truth; the brief is not.

## 2. The filter — the passband (what the second read is tuned to)

Three carriers the second reading passes, each tuned by a bookend:

1. **THE FORK** — every two-fold (A/not-A, wave/particle, matter/antimatter, +/−, true/false) read as the
   reader's **orientation**, not the world's. Tuned by the preface ("you are free") + Ch01 ("the labels
   are ours; the count is the world's").
2. **THE TURN** — every loop / sign / residue read as a **spinor turn**: it comes back signed; the residue
   is the −1 (the positron); the signs **compose** (the cocycle = conservation). Tuned by the coda's
   half-turn + the proved ±1 holonomies (Foucault, Bragg, Sagnac, the positron).
3. **THE TWO CEILINGS OF FREEDOM** — the **cruel laws** (the no-go walls bind: Hume, the demon, the
   inadmissibility walls, the arrow) and the **one room they leave** (the undetermined orientation — where
   will / meaning / God lives). Tuned by the preface's four doorways + Ch26 (determinism).

## 3. The craft rule — carry the band without disturbing the first read

Every loaded site must be **COMPLETE in the physics band** (stands alone as physics; the first read loses
nothing, feels nothing missing or cryptic) **AND carry the structure band** (surfaces under the filter).
Double-LAYER, never hidden-MESSAGE. Seeds are **felt, never announced** — no "read this again," no wink.
The first read must be honest and whole on its own; the second band must never make it feel incomplete.

## 4. The "PLAIN" guarantee — it must be EVIDENT there was more (not a buried Easter egg)

Three mechanisms make the second band unmistakable on re-read:

1. **The bookends frame it.** The preface NAMES free will; the coda DELIVERS it. The reader finishes
   *knowing* the book was about more than physics, and re-reads looking for it. (The frame announces a
   second band exists — without announcing where.)
2. **Density + recurrence.** The carriers (fork / turn / residue) appear in **every** chapter, so the
   pattern is a through-line, not one clue. The reader cannot re-read three chapters without seeing it.
3. **Marked payoff sites.** A handful of high-contrast lines that read flat first and **ring** second —
   the band's loudest points, impossible to pass the filter and miss. Known so far: Ch01 "the labels are
   ours; the count is the world's"; Ch16 "names the fork and takes no side"; Ch18 the positron's "−1";
   Ch26 the demon "fails twice over"; the preface "you are free" ⇄ the coda "the verdict is yours."

## 5. The keystone — Ch07 is the meta-site

The chapter that teaches **filtering and sampling (Fourier / Nyquist / the Dirichlet kernel)** is where, on
the second read, the reader realizes the book taught them *how to read it.* The second-pass filter is
itself a filter; the structure band sat **below the first read's passband** the whole time. This is the
single site that makes "there was more" not just felt but **understood** — plant the double layer here
most deliberately (still inside the rule: complete as signal-processing first; the recognition is the
reader's, never stated).
