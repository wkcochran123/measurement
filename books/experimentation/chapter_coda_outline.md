## 0. The idea, in one line

The Ch30 climax **already is** the speeder's trial (30.tex:177–235: speedometer = mechanics, GPS =
relativity, radar = quantum; the case turning from *is he speeding?* to *do you find him guilty?*; the
reader = the juror). The directive **generalizes that scene backward**: seed one short beat of the trial
at the end of every chapter, so the reader assembles the courtroom piece by piece across the whole book,
and Ch30 becomes the **verdict where the pieces converge** — not a metaphor sprung at the end, but the
payoff of a scene the reader has been building the entire way.

The coda is a **navigational handrail**: each one casts that chapter's central measurement lesson as a
step in the trial, giving a reader lost in the physics a concrete, recurring story to hold on to.

## 1. The load-bearing constraint — SEED, DO NOT SPOIL (carried-not-announced)

The Ch30 verdict is the book's climax and must stay the climax. The per-chapter codas **build the scene
but withhold the turn.** Withheld until Ch30, in every earlier coda:

- that the reader **is** the juror (early codas: the court, the bench, the record — never "you decide");
- that the question **turns with a sign** (the fact→verdict revolution, the ±1 / spinor 2π/4π geometry);
- that the verdict **stands on the continuum plank** (the continuity-of-time floor);
- the word the preface planted (**free will**) — stays under, per the preface's own discipline.

Early codas are *scene-setting*: the citation is written, the witnesses testify, the evidence is entered,
the frame question is raised (but not answered). The **first** time the case turns from fact to verdict,
and the first time a juror is addressed, is Ch30. A reader on the second pass sees the whole trial was
being built from Ch1; nothing is added on re-read, something only stops hiding — exactly the preface's
own move.

## 4. Guardrails the guard checks on every coda

1. **SEED-not-spoil / carried-not-announced** — no juror-address, no fact→verdict turn, no ±1 revolution, no continuum-as-verdict-floor, no "free will," before Ch30. §2 list is the withheld set.
2. **Four fences untouched** — NS / positron (proved-iff) / Hawking §5 (no named resolution) / Cantor–Gödel–Cohen (no metamathematics claimed). A coda that even brushes a fence STOPS and reports.
3. **Proved set frozen** — codas are prose; they add nothing to {±1 loop-holonomy, §5-Bell CHSH, positron iff, echo, Bragg, Dirac/chirality/Feynman ±1, Sagnac residue}. No new "proved."
4. **No grade inversion** — a coda never grades a chapter's effect above the floor the body assigned it.
5. **Translation layer (from the rethread)** — NO plan-vocabulary in the prose: no funge/tange, box/boxOf, the_capstone, electronVariation, NamingClose, .lean/"Lean"/Episode-NN, "axiom of choice." The band renders as physics ("the frame ours; the count the world's").
6. **Append, don't disturb** — the coda is appended at the chapter's end; the locked body prose is not rewritten. Visual marker consistent across chapters (a centered rule, like Ch30's).
7. **Length budget** — short. 1–2 tight paragraphs (≈60–140 words), lighter than Ch30's full scene; enough to cast the beat, not a new section. Ch30 stays the only full staging.
8. **Navigational, not decorative** — each coda must underscore *that chapter's* central aspect; if it only ornaments, it fails. The test: could a lost reader use this beat to place the chapter in the argument?
9. **Build clean** — LaTeX compiles; page delta reported; the coda-token sweep (juror/verdict/guilty/"you"/spinor) returns 0 before Ch30.
