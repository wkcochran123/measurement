## The one idea
The speeding ticket is not an illustration of the device — it **is** the device in
civic clothes. The first fact is a **hall-effect sensor going around and around at the
end of a gear train**: the wheel's rotation is the measurement loop's revolutions; the
sensor counting them is the reader reading the crank. "The expectation of a physical
payment is the **trace** of that fact" — the fine is what the raw rotation becomes,
read all the way through the instruments and the law. **It all goes back to the thing
going around.**

The load-bearing pun is **charge**: the physical charge is the loop count; the
financial charge is the fine; **you are charged the charge** — billed for the number of
times the wheel turned. The book's "charge = loop count" was always this.

## The three instruments (three independent reads of one speed — they must AGREE)
The fine is "consistent under the law" exactly when three instruments, calibrated
independently, land on the same number. Three readings forced to one value is the
device's agreement/pigeonhole made civic — the consensus record.

- **The hall-effect speedometer** (the car's own). Wheel → gear train → hall sensor →
  count → dial. The FIRST FACT (rotation). Calibration: tire circumference, gear ratio.
  Deepest floor: the quantum Hall effect is itself a *resistance standard* (von
  Klitzing) — the sensor's calibration bottoms out in a quantum invariant.
- **The radar gun** (the officer's). Doppler shift of an EM wave off the moving car.
  Calibration: the tuning-fork test, the internal reference frequency.
- **The GPS receiver** (the satellite system). Position-over-time from atomic clocks in
  orbit. Calibration: the *relativistic correction* (SR slows the moving clock, GR
  speeds the high-potential clock, ~38 µs/day net) — the instrument that literally
  cannot agree with the other two without Einstein.

## The arc — the story advances part by part (the instrument chain IS the curriculum)

continuously; state carries across codas. (Chapter→part boundaries below are the

- **Part I — Mechanics (≈Ch1–4).** The hall-effect speedometer's mechanical layer: the

  rotation, motion-and-its-record. The dial. Calibration: tire/gear.
- **Part II — Fields, Waves, Light (≈Ch5–8).** The radar gun enters: Doppler, the EM

  a measurement.

  transitions. GPS enters here. **Fence: Ch18 positron proved-iff, "not one inch more."**

- **Part VI — Foundations (≈Ch25–30).** The calibration of the calibration: how the

  full verdict scene, already written.

## The trace throughline (the spine every coda rides)
rotation (hall sensor) → the count → the speed → (three instruments agree, calibration
all the way down) → the fine. The fine is the trace of the rotation; charge = count =
what you owe. Every chapter's coda advances one link of this chain and re-sounds the
charge pun.

## The series coda (the outer frame)

The proof going around (the loop, the heartbeat = 2337) costs exactly as the wheel
going around incurs the fine. The heartbeat is the electricity bill of the needle. The
series-level coda is: you are charged for the loops, and so is the machine that proves
