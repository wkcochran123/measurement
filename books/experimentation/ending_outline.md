## 1. The spine

The ending changes from **a circle that closes** to **a loop that crosses itself and returns with a
sign.** Three movements. (i) **The recap — kept:** one difference → one instrument → six parts → four
ceilings → the proved handful (echo ±1, Bragg, the Dirac/chirality/Feynman ±1, the Sagnac residue, the
positron). This is the loop going *out*, and it stays as written. (ii) **The half-turn hinge —
replaces the old "circle closed":** the loop did not come back to where it started, because a count
that goes out and returns is a loop, and a loop that crosses itself comes back *oriented* — the closing
question wears the opening question's face, but a single turn brings it back with the opposite sign.
The old apex is demoted from climax to half-turn hinge. (iii) **The close, in the second person:** the
book carried both signs the whole way and fixed neither; which orientation is "matter," which sheet is
"+," which difference came first, was never the model's to set — it is the reader's frame, and the last
turn is theirs. The thing **carried the whole book and never named** is the **two-fold / orientation**
— matter vs antimatter, +/−, which-comes-first — the fork the reader silently picks every chapter. The
book hands it over; it never names it. **And the floor the close stands on is the book's own thesis:**
traced all the way down, the verdict rests not on a proved fact but on the assumption that *time runs
continuous* — the very continuum the book fenced as a shadow for 240 pages — so the reader, having seen
the finite-count alternative the book spent itself building, is the one left standing on the assumption.
**And the book's very last mark is Q.E.D.** — a gerundive (*Quod Erat Demonstrandum*, "that which is *to be*
demonstrated," not "done") — so the closing stamp is itself the assignment: the demonstration that remains is
handed to the reader.

### The staging: a speeder, three instruments, one verdict — resting on the physics already proved
The whole book replays in one scene: a driver clocked for speeding, three instruments that read the
speed, and a court that must return a verdict. **The three instruments ARE the book's three load-bearing
regimes**, and reconciling their three readings into one number is the crossing made concrete — three
strands, one verdict.
- **The speedometer — the car's own count (Part I, mechanics).** The origin→distance→speed velocity
  chain: the vehicle counting its own motion, reading speed from the inside. The book's first regime.
- **The GPS — true only by the relativistic clock correction (Part V, GR; Ch21).** The satellite clocks
  run fast by ~38 µs/day; without the general-relativistic correction (Pound–Rebka, already on the page

  because relativity is true.*
- **The radar gun — a Doppler measurement (Part IV, the quantum/photon; Part V for the conversion).** A
  photon emitted, reflected off the car, returned shifted; the shift read back as speed. The photon is
  the quantum regime; the shift-to-speed conversion is relativity. The radar reads true *only because
  the photon physics and the frame physics are true.*

Three readings of one speed. The court's job is to close them onto a single number — and to stand on
what makes each one trustworthy.
