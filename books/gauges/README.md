## The one bound (all four render THIS, and only this)

footprint** — the state `−1`, the three holonomy states `−1/0/+1` (count-to-three, no fourth), `1 = 0.999…` (the
residue closes → α dimensionless), and the deepest reading: **α = the electron's self-energy** (the Richardson
residual of the electron naming itself). But it does **not** produce the **magnitude** `137.036` — it **proves** the
magnitude is on the far side of its sight: a counter measures the trace (Ricci/volume) = the structure; the
magnitude lives in the trace-free part (Weyl, shape) a volume-counter is blind to. The **Dedekind bisection** draws
this to scale — the nines of `0.999…` bit by bit, halting at the floor — **bound = 60 bits**: structure near,
magnitude far.
