\chapter{The Ledgers}

% law's enforcement, made a table of record); the face-grade ledger

This book made two promises on its first page, and the audit of a
two-law book has two ledgers. Every claim traced to a name and a date:
the citation ledger, as in the volumes before. And --- new to this
volume, because its licence was new --- every picture listed beside the
citation it re-sees: the intuition ledger, which exists so that the
preface's law is checkable line by line. If any labeled intuition in
this book asserts one thing its listed neighbor does not already
contain, that row is a defect; the book asks to be audited against
exactly that standard.

\section*{The citation ledger}

\subsection*{Preface}
\begin{itemize}
\item Legendre, 1805 --- the method of least squares, published first;
Gauss, 1809 and 1821 --- the private prior use and the theory, for the
overdetermined systems of astronomy and geodesy. Back: the honest job of
many.
\item The trilateration arithmetic of the previous volume's preface,
carried forward as the earn's ground.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Chapter 1 --- The First Variation}
\begin{itemize}
\item Johann Bernoulli, 1696 --- the brachistochrone challenge; the
Newton solution overnight, by the account descended through his niece;
the cycloid; the five solvers.
\item Fermat, 1662 --- least time, the anticipation.
\item Euler, 1744 --- the treatise and the equation; Lagrange, 1755 and
1788 --- the variation itself and the \emph{M\'ecanique analytique},
with Euler's adoption and naming told as the attribution it is.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Chapter 2 --- The Second Variation}
\begin{itemize}
\item Legendre, 1786 --- the sign condition. \item Jacobi, 1837 ---
conjugate points. \item Morse, 1934 (\emph{The Calculus of Variations
in the Large}) --- critical points bounded below by topology; the index.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Chapter 3 --- The Cancellation}
\begin{itemize}
\item The schoolboy Gauss pairing, by his biographer's account ---
carried as story, flagged as such in the telling.
\item Black, 1946 --- the mutilated chessboard.
\item Noether, 1918 (\emph{Invariante Variationsprobleme}) --- symmetry
forces conservation; the G\"ottingen story with Hilbert's reply.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Chapter 4 --- The Squeeze}
\begin{itemize}
\item Archimedes (\emph{Measurement of a Circle}) --- the polygon
squeeze, the two fences standing at ninety-six sides.
\item Bolzano, 1817; Cauchy, 1821; Weierstrass --- the rigor backbone
(convergence certified without the limit; nested intervals).
\item Richardson, 1911; Richardson and Gaunt, 1927 --- the mesh as the
face's variation; the deferred approach to the limit.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Chapter 5 --- The Four as One}
\begin{itemize}

engine was this example.
\item Clairaut's relation --- via the standard geometry references this

\item The face-chapters' own citations, re-spent where each lamp was
lit.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Chapter 6 --- The Leak}
\begin{itemize}
\item Rayleigh, the 1870s (\emph{The Theory of Sound}) --- the
dissipation function, the annex beside the variational house.
\item The Navier--Stokes dissipative term --- named as the leak's
canonical address and as the series' owed face; cited as owed, spent
nowhere.
\end{itemize}

\section*{The intuition ledger}

Eleven labeled intuitions were handed over; each is listed with the
cited material it re-sees, and none adds a claim its neighbor lacks.

\begin{itemize}
\item[I1.] The soap film, the chain, the ray (Ch.~1) --- re-sees
Fermat's least time and the functionals of Euler and Lagrange.
\item[I2.] Stand-and-jiggle (Ch.~1) --- re-sees Lagrange's variation:
one arbitrary perturbation carrying all rivals.
\item[I3.] The mountain pass (Ch.~2) --- re-sees Legendre's condition
and Jacobi's classification: flatness at first order, truth at second.
\item[I4.] The bump under the carpet (Ch.~2) --- re-sees Morse's
invariance: what deformation relocates but cannot delete.
\item[I5.] The bookkeeper's zero (Ch.~3) --- re-sees the exact identity:
an exact cancellation certifies shared content.
\item[I6.] Pairing as seeing (Ch.~3) --- re-sees the rearrangement
identity of the Gauss story.
\item[I7.] The fly between the hands (Ch.~4) --- re-sees the squeeze:
caught means bracketed.
\item[I8.] The surveyor's stakes (Ch.~4) --- re-sees bracket
construction: swearing only to the between.
\item[I9.] Four lamps, four shadows (Ch.~5) --- re-sees the four cited
structures under one gesture; the frame of the worked example.
\item[I10.] The tesseract (Ch.~5) --- the series' image for
shadow-by-shadow knowledge; labeled ``It is an image,'' and it is
listed here as exactly that: an image, with no theorem beside it
because it asserts none.
\item[I11.] The forgetting (Ch.~6) --- re-sees dissipation's ring-down
(Rayleigh's annex); offered as a place to look; ``not a finding''
recorded here as its standing grade.
\end{itemize}

\section*{The face-grade ledger}

This volume used the four faces of mathematics as proof structures, and
each stands anchored to a cited summit: the extremal face on
Euler--Lagrange; the obstruction face on Morse; the cancellation face on
Noether; the convergence face on the line from Archimedes to
Weierstrass. Its two device appearances are graded as their exhibits
stated: the second chapter's, a shape-resemblance with the obstruction
reading owed; the fourth chapter's, a carried practice --- the
bracketing --- with the limit owed. The boundary of the method was named
in the sixth chapter and is a grade in itself: the three clean faces
leak at dissipation, the fourth survives, and the territory past the
boundary belongs to the volume that pays the fluid its debt --- the
face that remains, here as in every volume of this series, owed. The
unity of the four is graded exactly as sold: a reading.

\section*{The closing line}

A book of intuitions succeeds when its reader can no longer tell, from
the inside, which understanding arrived by proof and which by picture
--- and its honesty consists in the ledgers that can always tell. The
mathematics belongs to the names above; the pictures re-see it and add
nothing; the arrangement is this book's only claim, and the arrangement
is a reading plan, as it was in the volume before. The four faces are
now felt as well as checked. What waits past the boundary line --- the
leak's own volume, the fluid's unpaid station --- waits with its debt
recorded in three ledgers now. The feeling is yours; the receipts are
here.
