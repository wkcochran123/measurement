## Chapters (each: the intuition offered → the cited structure → the fence)

### Ch 1 — The First Variation (geometry's face: the extremal structure)
- INTUITION: the soap film, the hanging chain, the light ray — nature's
  answers look chosen; the feeling that "the true path is the best path."
- CITED STRUCTURE: proof by first variation — vary the candidate, demand
  the first-order change vanish (Euler 1744, Lagrange 1755/1788; the
  brachistochrone story at Cavendish grade — Bernoulli's 1696 challenge);
  geodesics as Vol 2's Ch7 objects re-felt.
- FENCE: "best" is a property of the functional chosen, not a purpose in
  the world (teleology refused, one paragraph).

### Ch 2 — The Second Variation (topology's face: the obstruction structure)
- INTUITION: the pass between two valleys — first variation zero, and yet
  unstable; the feeling that flatness at first order hides the real story.
- CITED STRUCTURE: proof by second variation — stability, conjugate points
  (Jacobi 1837; Legendre's condition); and the topological reading:
  invariants as what NO deformation can remove (the obstruction; Morse
  1934 tying critical points to topology — the face's deepest citation).
- FENCE + LENS SITE 1 (proposed): the device's residue offered as an
  INTUITION-exhibit of obstruction-shape (illustration, not identity; the
  same two-fence formula as Vol 2's exhibits).

### Ch 3 — The Cancellation (algebra's face: the identity structure)
- INTUITION: the bookkeeper's zero — two tellings of one till; the feeling
  that a perfect cancellation is never an accident.
- CITED STRUCTURE: proof by exact identity — telescoping, invariants,
  conservation as cancellation (Noether 1918 as the face's summit: the
  variational symmetry that FORCES the conservation; the bridge between
  this face and Ch1's, cited whole).
- FENCE: exactness is the algebra's property; the Wheatstone-style null
  (Vol 1's method) referenced as the series' own instance, one clause.

### Ch 4 — The Squeeze (numerics' face: the convergence structure)
- INTUITION: the two hands closing on the fly; the feeling that an answer

- CITED STRUCTURE: proof by squeeze — nested intervals, monotone
  convergence (Weierstrass; Cauchy's criteria; Archimedes' polygons as the
  ur-instance, told as story); discrete variation — vary the mesh, watch
  the bracket (Richardson 1911 cited for the refinement idea).
