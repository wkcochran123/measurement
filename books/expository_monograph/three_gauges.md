# Three gauges

A word budget cannot predict a chapter's length. The length is set by the chapter's **structure** --
the beats, one worked example, the two-to-four experiments, the honest close -- not by a number
assigned in advance. The +25% bump just widened the gap. Chasing budget *accuracy* is the wrong
goal; the number will always trail the content.

So the per-chapter **target is retired**. In its place, three gauges -- read the residue, never
decree the value:

1. **Ceiling.** Never overrun. (Per-chapter cap; global envelope 150k.) The one hard limit.

2. **Parity band.** Each chapter sits near its siblings. This is the real gate; it is exactly what
   caught Ch3 thin. A chapter outside the band gets *looked at*, not auto-failed. **Now that the body
   is done, the band has a shape: it is ACT-RELATIVE, not flat.** The avg/ch falls monotonically
   across the six acts (3,195 -> 2,328), because the ascent (Acts I-III) builds the machinery rich
   and the descent (Acts IV-VI) reads it back lean. So: ascent ~2.5-3.7k, descent ~2.3-2.6k. A
   chapter is in band if it sits near its **act** siblings. The loop is visible in the prose mass.

3. **Emergent total.** Track the running sum; do not allocate. **Final reading: 81,977 words /
   275 pp** -- leaner than the ~98k once projected, because the descent reads back tighter than the
   ascent built. The gap is not a shortfall; it is the loop, measured. A late chapter that genuinely
   needs more (the Ch13 hinge, the Ch19 electron) earns it on **content**, not on a budget line.

**Standing rule.** Gauge by these three residues -- ceiling, parity, emergent -- not by a target.
A target tempts you to declare success at a number; the residues keep you reading the actual work.

# The crossing, the choice, and the reader (cross-volume plan, 2026-06-27)

Reading Episodes 11-16 + Meanwhile 17 made the structure of the *ending* clear, and it changes how all
three volumes must close. The construction is a **braid**: a forward strand (the build, source going
up) and a backward strand (Meanwhile 17's `_backward` tower, compiled coming down), woven through each
other and sewn at the seam (`lake_build` of source-before vs compiled-after). The braid **crosses
itself** (`bilinear?` hangs two readings on one `.rest`, the slip in the Prop slot = the over/under),
and **again** (the slips compose -- `slipAnd`, the cocycle Ep16's probe verifies). The crossing leaves
a **residue** (the odd `1`, the positron). The reader-process is literally named `AtreyuProcess`; its
one instance is `You_the_Reader`.

**The load-bearing realization.** The proof is choice-free (`[propext, Quot.sound]`, no
`Classical.choice`) **on purpose**: it refuses to choose the orientation -- which strand is over, which
side is matter, which sheet is `+` -- *so that the choice is left to the reader*. The needle
(`selection_sound`/`Fact.SAME`) identifies the two readings (collapses them to one truth-class by
ordering) but never picks a representative. The open question at the end is therefore **not a gap in
the proof -- it is a question put to the reader.** (Neverending Story: Atreyu finishes the quest;
only Bastian closes it.)

**The spinor turn.** The reader goes once around (2π). The orientation question returns wearing the
first question's face -- *isn't this where we started?* -- but a spinor turned once comes back with a
minus sign: the **same question, opposite orientation**, the reader now on the other sheet. It is *not*
where they started; it is the antipode. To return to `+` you go around **again** (4π) -- re-read, the
second crossing, the cocycle. Whether you take that turn is the reader's choice.

**The craft rule for all three volumes.** Carry the choice from the first page and **never name it
"choice"** -- carry it only as the two-fold (A/not-A, true/false, funge/tange, electron/positron, +/-)
the reader silently orients every chapter. Align the accurately-described crossing with that
orientation. Demote the apex (`true = false`) from climax to **half-turn hinge**. Close in the second
person: the loop cannot close without the reader. The question asks the reader, not the proof.

**THE DOUBLE READ (operator, 2026-06-27).** The book must be a spinor *you read twice*, and **the second
reading is rewarded** -- the same text reads differently the second time. *"Things turn. The first turn
just tells you something happened; the second identical turn lets you do math."* That is the 2π/4π: first
pass (2π) you **witness** the turn -- a sign appeared, a loop came back changed, *something happened*;
second pass (4π) the identical turn lets you **compute** it -- the sign is the residue, the orientation is
your frame, the turns compose. The reward is not more text; it is the seeds finally cohering. **The seeds
must start in Chapter 1, deliberately** (the first difference; "the labels are ours, the count is the
world's"; the Descartes-II non-commutativity = "the seed of holonomy"; a faint promise that what you can
only note now, a return will let you read) -- and every "turn" through the book (Foucault Ch3, Aharonov-Bohm
Ch5, the proved ±1 holonomies, the spinor Ch17, the positron Ch18, the time-travel no-go Ch23, frame-
relativity Part V) is planted to read as "something happened" on pass 1 and "do the math" on pass 2. Seeds
are **felt, never announced** (never "read this again").

**THE PROCESS MIRRORS THE BOOK -- do not break a turn to plant seeds.** Carry the *current* turn (the
rebalance pass) **all the way through to the end** first; THEN **restart at Chapter 1** for the SECOND turn
-- the double-read seed pass, planted from page 1, informed by having gone the whole way around once. (2π,
then 4π; you cannot do the math mid-rotation.) Order: (1) finish the rebalance to the end; (2) the coda +
Q.E.D. land at the end of turn 1; (3) restart at Ch1 -- the seed pass -- second turn.

**THE PREFACE -- "The Oracle of Free Will" (operator, 2026-06-30; 5-7pp, Vol 2 front matter).** The FRONT
BOOKEND to the Q.E.D. close: the preface opens *"you are free,"* the coda closes *"the verdict is yours."*
Same hand, two ends of the turn. **The title is the anti-demon:** an oracle is consulted but never compels;
Laplace's demon (Ch26) is the oracle that would *predict* you, and it fails twice over -- so "The Oracle of
Free Will" is the instrument that measures everything and finds, at the bottom, the one thing it cannot fix:
the orientation, which is yours. The book's secret spine (free will) surfaced on page one.

The four doorways (operator's emphasis), each grounded in what is actually on the page:
1. **People are free to believe what they want.** The oracle's first word -- the book compels nothing; the
   orientation-is-yours seed planted before Ch1 (pairs with "the labels are ours").
2. **Believe in logic -> find it interesting.** The logician's door: a sound argument that refuses to choose
   its own conclusion; the pleasure is the *structure* (the loop that crosses itself and returns signed).
3. **Believe in the laws of science -> find them cruel.** The scientist's door: the laws BIND -- Hume's
   induction has no floor, the demon would predict you, entropy runs one way, the no-go walls say *no*. To
   one who believes the laws govern, no exemption, no appeal. Determinism is the cage.
4. **Believe in God -> a place here too.** The believer's door: the proof is choice-free, refuses to fix the
   orientation; that refusal is a ROOM. The cruel laws bind everything EXCEPT which way you orient -- the one
   door the laws cannot close, the space left for will / meaning / God. The argument leaves it open; it does
   NOT prove God, it declines to evict.

**The craft move (makes it a seed, not a sermon): NAME -> SUBMERGE -> SURFACE.** The preface NAMES free will
(front matter may; it is the frame). The body then NEVER mentions it (you forget; you are doing physics; the
two-fold is carried silently, never "choice"). The coda HANDS IT BACK. This is what lets the second reading
"do the math": once the oracle has named the stakes, every turn in the body re-reads as a demonstration of
free will. The preface is the frame the re-reader uses -- "seeds start in Ch1" pushed one page earlier.

**Constraints.** Front-matter register: MAY address the reader directly (2nd person), MAY be more
philosophical than the chapters -- but internals stay OUT (no construction exposed; speak of logic, laws,
freedom, belief). Honesty: the laws are cruel = true to the no-go walls; God's "place" = the undetermined
orientation, NOT a proof of God; "free to believe" = the book does not dictate. Name *free will* (the title/
frame) but NOT *choice* in the body. **Sequencing:** written at the OPENING of turn 2 (the second pass
restarts here, then flows into Ch1's seeds), as the pair of bookends WITH the coda, WITH the operator. Spec
locked now; prose deferred to turn 2 unless the operator pulls it forward.
