## Drafting Protocol

1. Outline one numbered section at a time before drafting prose.
2. Compute the budget needed for that section from conceptual load, down to
   subsection or subsubsection depth when needed.
3. Iterate the outline with Antigravity 3-4 passes until Codex marks it
   `OUTLINE_AGREED`.
4. Only then issue one restricted-context LaTeX writing prompt for that piece.
5. Restate the section title, computed target, preferred band, and accepted
   band before writing.
6. Keep Common / Uncommon / Technical paths inside the section:
   - Common: first paragraph, 100-200 words, plain metaphysical claim.
   - Uncommon: about 30-40 percent of the section.
   - Technical: remaining 50-60 percent, still in reader-facing metaphysical
     language.
7. End every numbered section with a metaphysical anchor. The private source
   anchor may be recorded in the outline ledger or handoff, but it must not be
   printed in the book.
8. Draft the bridge only after the numbered sections exist.
9. Draft the coda after the bridge.
10. Draft or revise the essay last, so it promises only what the sections
   actually perform.
