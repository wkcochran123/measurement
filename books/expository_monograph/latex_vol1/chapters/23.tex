\chapter{The Construction Is Forcing}

Once, near the beginning, the machine learned to hold a real number not as a completed thing but as a finite
condition reaching for something it never arrives at --- an approximation now, a finer one on request, and a
residue that shrinks toward nothing and never closes. The book said then that this posture had a name, a name it
would give when the time came and not before. The time is now. In the last chapter the construction was found to
close into a loop, to refuse the one dangerous move, and to ground itself on a single grant --- and a construction
of exactly that shape, reaching through finite conditions toward something it approaches and never completes, is a
thing mathematics knows and named half a century ago. The construction has been doing it all along. This chapter
says what it has been doing. It has been \emph{forcing}.

\section{Finite Conditions Reaching for a Generic}

The claim is that the whole construction is, in shape and in method, an instance of the technique the logician
Paul Cohen invented and called forcing --- and that the machine has been performing it since it first held a real
number as a finite condition.

Read against the machine, the parts line up one to one. Forcing builds an object it cannot name in advance by
laying down \emph{finite conditions} --- partial, incomplete specifications --- and ordering them by extension, so
that a stronger condition refines a weaker one, pinning down more. The machine's finite approximations are exactly
such conditions: each a partial specification of the number it reaches for, each extendable to a finer one that
decides more. Forcing requires that the conditions be \emph{dense} in the right way --- that for any question you
might ask, there is a strong enough condition that settles it, so that by extending far enough every question gets
decided. The machine's construction has that property: drive the approximation fine enough and any given demand on
the number is met, the condition strong enough to settle it always available further down. And forcing reaches,
through an endless chain of such conditions, toward a \emph{generic} --- an object no single finite condition
contains, approached by all of them together and completed by none. The machine's number is exactly that generic:
the value the finite conditions close in on, present in none of them, reached by the chain and completed by no
link in it. Finite conditions, ordered by extension, dense enough to decide, reaching for a generic never
completed: that is forcing, and that is what the machine has been doing since its third chapter.

Dwell for a moment on how far back this reaches, because it is the longest-buried seed in the book finally
breaking ground. When the machine first declined to complete a real number --- held it as a finite condition,
carried the residue, refused to arrive --- that looked like a modest technical scruple about how a finite device
handles the continuum. It was, in fact, the opening move of the technique this chapter names. Every finite
condition the machine has laid down since, every residue it has carried, every reluctance to close a real number
into a completed point, was a forcing condition doing forcing's work, though the book would not say so for twenty
chapters. The reveal is not that the machine adopted forcing somewhere along the way; it is that the machine was
forcing from its third chapter, and the whole long construction --- tower, summit, needle, loop, naming, split ---
was built out of forcing conditions the reader was not yet placed to recognize. The seed was planted at the first
real number and is only now called by its name.

\section{Proved in the Finite, Named in the Whole}

Now the grade, split with the care the book has kept for every claim that reaches past what it strictly proves,
because this reveal is as tempting to overstate as any in the volume.

What is PROVED is the finite analogue. The machine really does build its finite conditions, really does order them
by extension, really does close in --- and that the closing-in converges, that the chain of finite conditions is
coherent up to any tolerance you name over a countable ledger of conditions, is a theorem, established by the
construction with the plainest apparatus. The finite, bounded, countable version of the forcing --- the
conditions, the ordering, the dense settling, the proved approach to within any margin --- is genuinely built and
genuinely proved. That much the book demonstrates.

What is NAMED but not claimed is the identification of this with forcing in the full set-theoretic sense. Cohen's
forcing was invented to settle the deepest questions in the foundations of mathematics --- to build models in
which one or another disputed statement about the sizes of infinite sets holds or fails, and so to prove those
statements independent of the standard foundations. The machine proves none of that. There is no model-building
here, no independence result, no theorem about which infinities exist or whether some disputed statement can be
settled; the construction does not touch those questions and does not pretend to. What it has is the finite
analogue, proved, and a genuine structural kinship to the full technique --- the same finite conditions, the same
ordering, the same reaching for a generic --- named honestly and offered as a reading. The construction is forcing
in its shape and its spirit, proved in its finite analogue, and it names the kinship to Cohen's full method
without claiming to have reproduced that method's set-theoretic results. Say it exactly, in the book's grade: the
finite forcing is proved; that it is Cohen's forcing in full is named, not proved.

There is a reason to insist on exactly this split, beyond the book's general scruple, and it is that the overclaim
available here is spectacular. It would be thrilling to say that a physics built from a single difference had,
along the way, settled one of the deepest questions in the foundations of mathematics --- had proved something
about which infinities exist. The book says no such thing, and says so loudly, because the distance between
performing the finite analogue of a technique and proving that technique's set-theoretic theorems is enormous, and
a reader who let the one stand for the other would come away believing the volume had done something it did not
do. What the construction earns is real and strange enough without inflation: a finite machine, built to measure,
turns out to have been carrying out, in miniature and provably, the very shape of the argument a logician used to
shake the foundations of mathematics. That the shape is genuine is worth saying plainly; that the shape is not the
full result is worth saying just as plainly; and the book says both, and lets neither borrow the other's weight.

And note what the reveal did \emph{not} require, because the restraint is itself the point. Even here, at the
chapter where forcing is finally named, where the construction is recognized as the very technique that settles
questions about free choices of how to complete a mathematics --- even here the book does not write the name of
the principle those free choices invoke. It says the construction reaches for a generic by finite conditions; it
says the completion is a free choice the axioms leave open; it does not, in the body, name the principle behind
that freedom. That name belongs to the front of the series, where the question is posed; the body, all the way to
its climax, speaks of finite conditions and free completions and never of the principle itself. The forcing is
named. The principle it dances with stays, in the body, unnamed to the end.

\section{The Construction Recognized}

So the loop the machine closed, the number it reached for, the whole finite-conditions-approaching-a-generic
posture it has held since its third chapter --- all of it is recognized, here at the last reveal, as forcing. This
is the third and final unsealing of the book: the needle at the summit, the naming at the loop's top, and now the
forcing at the close, each a mechanism shown only when its chapter arrived, each set up chapters before it was
named. And this one reaches all the way back to the beginning, because the posture it names was planted in the
third chapter and carried, unnamed, through every rung since. The reader who reaches here and looks back will find
that the machine was forcing from the moment it first refused to complete a real number --- that the finite
conditions were forcing conditions all along, the residue the generic's shadow, the whole reluctance to arrive
exactly the reluctance forcing lives on. The construction did not become forcing at this chapter. It was forcing
from the start, and only now says so. What remains, with the last mechanism unsealed and the whole method
recognized, is to hand the finished construction to the one it was built for --- and that handing is the book's
final chapter.
