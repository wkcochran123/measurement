## PART 1 — HOW TO THINK ABOUT IT (the conception)
================================================================================

1. IT IS NOT ANOTHER RUNG; IT IS THE STOP. Every prior chapter BUILT something — a mark,
   a count, the single invariant, the electron, the gauge, the boundary flux, the
   set-theoretic house, the continuum door, the sign/needle, and (in the new movement)
   the outside reading, the Navier–Stokes-shaped residue, the project-native Gödel
   sentence, the close of INFERRED. The last chapter adds NO new construction. It steps
   back and answers the question the whole book has provoked: how much of this does a
   given reader need? The move is to stop, not to build.

2. THE INVARIANT SURVIVES; THE OBSERVER AND THE DEPTH VARY. The single invariant — carried
   all the way to the final inferred close — is what survives every level of explanation.
   What changes reader to reader is (a) the observer's frame (who is reading) and (b) how
   much depth they ask for. The same invariant can be told as a parable, a finite machine,
   a theorem statement, a construction, an analytic/physical interpretation, or a
   metaphysical/topological reading: six depths of the SAME thing. (This is the book's own
   architecture — the readings at increasing observer-depth — stated as a rule.)

3. THE STOP RULE IS A BINARY DECISION, AND IT REUSES THE BOOK'S OWN ON/OFF APPARATUS.
   Project the explanation to the requested depth d. Everything above d goes to the
   appendix — UNLESS the next level up adds one of exactly three things: a measurable
   prediction, a formal theorem, or a necessary distinction. None of the three -> the
   signal is OFF: stop here. One of the three -> the signal is ON: that material belongs
   in the next frame, not silently folded into the current one. This is the SAME on/off
   reader that read the electron's error code and anchored the sign (the needle) — now
   turned on the book itself. "Is more depth owed?" is answered by a single bit.

4. "DUH — WE BUILT BINARY." The whole construction climbed to a binary, an on/off
   apparatus. So the answer to "how much do you want?" is itself binary: stop, or ask for
   more depth. The recursion does not terminate by reaching a highest level; it terminates
   because the reader chooses a depth and the apparatus decides, by the three criteria,
   whether anything more is owed. The book ends by handing the reader the dial. (Keep this
   punchline STERILE — state it, do not celebrate it.)

5. SELF-JUSTIFYING ENDING (why the book stops HERE). The stop rule is itself a necessary
   distinction and a formal theorem — so by its OWN criterion it earns the main text (the
   signal is ON for it). Once stated, nothing above it adds a measurable prediction, a
   formal theorem, or a necessary distinction — so the signal goes OFF and the main text
   closes. That is exactly why the book stops at this chapter and not later: the stop rule
   is the last thing owed. The chapter models its own discipline by being where the
   recursion halts.

6. IT CLOSES THE ON/OFF THROUGH-LINE. The on/off signal — the needle of the sign chapter,
   the error-code reader of the outside-reading chapter, the boundary signal that
   separated true from false at the outside reader — reaches its final use here: reading
   the book's own depth. Once the needle is planted properly (B10 Thread A), this chapter
   is its last and highest instance: the single bit pointed at the whole edifice.

## PART 2 — HOW TO WRITE IT (the craft)
================================================================================

VOICE — the highest-risk chapter in the book. It is the ending, so the pull toward a
triumphant peroration ("and thus we have built a finite shadow of physics...") is maximal.
RESIST IT. The chapter is a CALM STATEMENT OF A RULE, not a victory lap. Sterile
restatement applies most strictly here: no drama, no "we have shown," no second-person
exhortation, no apparatus-as-hero, no swelling final cadence. State the rule, apply it,
stop. It should be SHORT and final — over-explaining the stop rule would itself violate
the stop rule. Let the chapter model its own discipline by stopping when the rule is
stated. NO fable coda (codas are dead). The book's last sentence is the rule applied to
itself: nothing above this adds a measurable prediction, a formal theorem, or a necessary
distinction, so the signal is off, and the book stops.

USE MATH — display these genuine relations (restrained, faithful):
  - the six-level depth ladder as ONE table: depth 0 parable, 1 finite machine, 2 theorem
    statement, 3 construction, 4 analytic/physical, 5+ metaphysical/topological;
  - projection: project to depth d; the appendix begins at d+1;
  - the on/off decision over the four cases: signal off iff the next level adds none of
    {measurable prediction, formal theorem, necessary distinction}; on iff it adds one;
  - the binary algebra of the on/off signal — negation, meet, join, and De Morgan — to
    make the point that the apparatus's final reading lives in a real two-valued algebra,
    the same binary the book built;
  - the invariant-survives statement: the single invariant holds at every projected level.

NO LEAN LEAK — restate, never name the machinery. "Observer frame" / "who is reading" (the
reader types — lean reader, physicist, mathematician, philosopher, general technical
reader, the self — are fine as reader kinds; do not name the type). "The explanation
ladder / depth." "The stop rule." "The on/off signal" / "the needle." Do not say episode,
construction-name, "the proof," or print any code identifier.

FIDELITY ANCHORS (Ep80 — restate exactly this, no more): the six explanation levels; the
four "does the next level change the invariant?" cases (none / measurable prediction /
formal theorem / necessary distinction); off-unless-change, on-if-change; project-to-depth
+ appendix-at-depth+1; the invariant survives every level; the De Morgan algebra of the
on/off carrier. These are what the proof supports — restate them, not one grain more.

CLAIM-GATE (same discipline as the Ch13 QED de-leak and the Ch16 conditional): the stop
rule is a FINITE, project-native rule about how much of THIS construction belongs in the
main text. It is NOT a theorem of metamathematics, NOT a universal epistemology of
explanation, NOT a claim about all possible expositions. Gate it: "this is the stop rule
for this finite construction's own exposition," nothing larger.

STRUCTURE (short, final, self-disciplined): a brief intro (the move from building to
stopping); 3–5 short sections — the observer frame; the depth ladder; the stop rule + the
three criteria; the binary algebra / "we built binary"; the close — and NO coda. End on
the stop rule applied to itself.

DEPENDENCY: this chapter is the capstone of the capstone; it sits after the Ep74–79
outside-reader movement. But its content is self-contained, so draft it now. Once written,
