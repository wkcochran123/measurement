# Measurement (Volume 1) — Budget Outline (the implementation budget)

*Stage-2 deliverable of task `vol1`. Where `outline_working.md` fixes WHAT each chapter says (and pins it to the
build), this fixes HOW MANY WORDS it gets. Mirrors the pattern of `outline_budget.md` (the old-Vol-1 budget).
**Owned/gated by Kodo; Podo drafts to it.** Pairs with `outline_working.md` (24 chapters, 6 acts). Podo PROPOSES
the envelope below — Kodo scales/gates.*

## Mechanism (how this budget is used)
- **Global envelope:** one target total; every line below is a division of it.
- **Quantum:** allocations in **250-word steps** (openers in 250s too). No target is an arbitrary number.
- **Two quanta per chapter:** an **opener** (the continuing-story / speeder essay before §N.1) and a **body**
  (the numbered sections). **Pivotal openers get 750; the rest 500.** Pivotal = the reveal/keystone chapters:
  **Ch1** (the first difference), **Ch11** (the needle), **Ch13** (the backward walk opens the return),
  **Ch18** (the naming), **Ch19** (the predicted split), **Ch23** (the forcing reveal), **Ch24** (the close).
- **Section target:** within a chapter, the body splits across its sections at the section's `OUTLINE_AGREED`
  gate (≈ body ÷ #sections, re-quantized to 250).
- **Conservation:** sections sum to the chapter, chapters to the act, acts + front matter to the allocated
  total; allocated + reserve = envelope. Overruns repaid from reserve or a named sibling — the envelope never
  drifts.
- **Band at the gate:** drafting carries the section budget as a soft band (±10%); review checks the count.

## Sizing rationale (proposed target — Kodo to gate)
Vol 2 landed at **360 pp for 30 ch + prefaces** (~12 pp/ch of physics exposition). Vol 1 is **24 ch of the
CONSTRUCTION** — terser per claim (the claims are code-carried: a theorem name + a footprint, not a physics
narrative), so ~**10 pp/ch** is honest, not padded. **Load-weighted** (Kodo's steer): the definitional runs
(Act II) are LIGHTEST; the apex (Act III, the needle), the naming + predicted split (Act V), and the reader/
forcing close (Act VI) carry MORE. Proposed envelope **112,000 words ≈ ~250 pp** (at ~450 w/p, the old-Vol-1
realized density). *If a fuller construction is wanted, scale the envelope toward ~265–288 pp (Vol-2-proportionate)
— the per-chapter weights hold; multiply the bodies.*
