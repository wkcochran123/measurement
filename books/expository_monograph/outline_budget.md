## Mechanism (how this budget is used)
- **Global envelope:** one target total; every line below is a division of it.
- **Quantum:** allocations are in 250-word steps (openers in 250s too). No section
  target is an arbitrary number.
- **Two quanta per chapter:** an **opener** (the speeder's-trial essay before §X.1)
  and a **body** (the numbered sections). Pivotal openers (Prologue, Ch13, Ch19,
  Ch32) get 750; the rest 500.
- **Section target:** within a chapter, the body is split across its sections at the
  section's `OUTLINE_AGREED` gate (planned split ≈ body ÷ #sections, re-quantized to
  250). The gate is where a section's number becomes binding.
- **Conservation:** sections sum to the chapter, chapters to the part, parts +
  front/coda/appendices to the allocated total; allocated + reserve = envelope.
  Overruns are repaid from the reserve or by trimming a named sibling — the envelope
  never drifts.
- **Band at the gate:** drafting carries the section budget as a soft band (±10%);
  review checks the count; out-of-band returns the section.
