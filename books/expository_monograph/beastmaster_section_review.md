### THREAD A — THE NEEDLE is never introduced, yet claimed as a through-line (L2, K)
The single classical decision ("the needle") is the book's spine, and it is handled
backwards at BOTH ends:
- FIRST APPEARANCE: Ch11 §2 line 168 — "the needle it sits in rather than derives" —
  dropped in apposition to "a single classical choice," with NO definition or setup, as
  if already known.
- FALSE THROUGH-LINE: Ch17 claims it has been carried since the start, three times —
  17:51 "the needle the book has carried from its first pages"; 17:303 "since its first
  mark"; 17:328 "visible at every stage" + 17:331–334 a step-by-step rewrite asserting it
  was carried from Ch1 onward.
- Result: the needle is assumed at Ch11 and back-claimed to Ch1, but planted NOWHERE in
  Ch1–10. This cuts directly against the book's own honesty thesis.
- FIX (resolves K-NEEDLE from B9): plant the needle in Ch1 (it is already in the preface),
  introduce it by name there, let it recur lightly at each classical-choice site
  (Ch11 §2 gets a real referent), so Ch17's "since the first mark" becomes TRUE. Cleaner
  than deleting the Ch17 claims. Either way, Ch11 §2 needs a one-line introduction and the
  Ch17 ×3 claims must match whatever Ch1 actually plants.

### THREAD B — THE SINGLE INVARIANT is used in Ch9 before Ch8 delivers it (L2, K)
Ch9 opens (9:3–5) "Chapter 8 closed with a named invariant... the second variation, read
as the energy-squared diagonal of a positive-definite pairing," and leans on it again at
9:17, 9:98, 9:500. Ch8 as built (§1 energy-squared, §2 boundary accounting, §3 "The
Boundary Reads the Load") NEVER states that identity — §3 (turn 203) was explicitly told to
stop before it. Until the operator-ruled Ch8 S04/S05 land the displayed identity
(second variation = mixed coupling = energy diagonal = the single invariant), every Ch9
back-reference above is leaning on air. This is the titular concept; it must land in Ch8.
