# Act I-II Budget Review

Summary:
- Acts I-II sit inside the Revision 4 ascent band; ceiling is not the live constraint.
- Ch1 is top-edge but earned as the cold-open primitive and first modeling fence.
- Ch4 and Ch7 carry the strongest compression risk: many bridges at low-to-mid counts.
- Ch5 has the clearest padding risk: repeated physics-fence language around local present, gauge, and superposition.
- Ch8 is adequate but should be audited for claim drift around the six-act frame and the provocative true=false hinge.
