# Act V-VI Budget Review

Summary line 1: All ten chapters sit inside the governing descent neighborhood, so numeric length is not the main problem.
Summary line 2: Most chapters earn their space with one central outside-reading or return-to-reader move plus a compact accounting close.
Summary line 3: The largest adequacy risk is claim density, especially where finite facts receive Einstein, Navier-Stokes, nonlocality, or cosmology names.
Summary line 4: The clearest padding risk is Ch32, where the reader-as-final-apparatus close circles the same address several times.
Summary line 5: Recommended disposition is keep for most rows, claim-drift audit for Ch26, and internal rebalance for Ch30 and Ch32.
