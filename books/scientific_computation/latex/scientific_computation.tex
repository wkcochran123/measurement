\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This is the reading gauge. Volumes~1 through~3 build the instrument --- in mathematics, in physics, in
computation. This volume explains it, to anyone, and hands you the means to check it yourself. It reads as an
out-of-order history of experimental science: the ideas are arranged by how the machine uses them, not by when they
were discovered.

The method is a discipline of decorrelation. Each result is told four ways that share no vocabulary --- a
mathematical account that uses no mathematics, a physical account that uses no computation, a computational account
that uses no physics, and a literal walk through the code. Three registers that cannot borrow each other's words,
plus the code itself: their agreement is the argument. When four descriptions that could not have coordinated land on
the same thing, the thing is hard to fake.

There is one rule of honesty here, and it is carried in the citations rather than announced. Where the text leans on
settled science it names, generously, the people whose experiments and theorems it stands on, so the reader rests on
known ground. Where the citations thin out, something new is happening, with no prior name to cite; that silence is
the signal to slow down and check. Generosity, then, is an instrument --- it makes the novel legible by contrast.

What follows walks the whole instrument, in order of how the machine uses each idea: from the first told-apart
difference to the coupling it can only bracket. The tone stays light throughout; the volume opens on a doesn't-matter
particle hunting for posit-wrong ones, and never entirely puts the joke down --- because the joke keeps turning out
to be the structure. The ending is the climax, not a disappointment: the machine measures to its own counting floor
and reports the bracket it can repeat.

A single note about the artifact, made once and then set aside. Everything in this book is not a thought experiment:
it is built, and machine-checked, in a formal proof assistant (Lean), so that every claim here can be run and
re-run by anyone, and each returns the same answer. That is the whole reason to trust a bracket rather than a story.
But the implementation is not the subject. From here on this book describes the \emph{instrument} --- the
construction and what it measures --- not the language it happens to be written in. When we say ``the machine'' we
mean the instrument; the checkable artifact stands behind every line, silently, and you are always free to go run it.
