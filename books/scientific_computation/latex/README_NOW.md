## What the device is

It is **the model of the electron** — and be precise about both words.

- **A model**, not the world and not a derivation of it. It reads the electron in its own terms (a
  distinction counted, a residue that won't cancel, a self-energy carried to an open bracket) and it
  can prove real things *about the representation* — choice-free, fenced, reproducible. It never claims
  to *be* the physics. Its number is `α_c` (the compiler structure constant), its own; the laboratory's
  `137.036` sits outside it, named once and never captured.
- **The** model, not *a* model among many. To model anything at all is one act: draw a distinction,
  count it, carry the residue, name the fixed point. There is no second structure of representation.
  So the device isn't competing with other models; it's that one act performed purely. And that is
  *why* it speaks only of the electron: a model holds **one** distinction at a time. The electron is
  the single reading it holds — not a scope it could exceed. **Do not extrapolate the electron-reading
  to the nucleus, to other particles, or to "everything."** That crossing is unearned scope — the
  crank move the whole project exists to refuse.

## The conclusion (this is what your preface is really about)

A model is only the **contravariant** half of the act: the naming, the reading, the question pulled
back toward what would answer it. By construction it can only ever produce *its own names*. It cannot
supply the world's answer. To close the loop — to let the world change the unresolved slot so the name
is *earned* rather than *planted* — you need the **covariant** half: something that acts in and is
answered by the world. Observation. An apparatus. A body. **A robot.**

This is exactly your two-pass calibration: *you may choose the question; you may not choose the answer.*
The second pass — the world allowed to answer, the record that survives the exchange of roles — is the
covariant arrow the model cannot draw from inside itself. Your "built / measured / interpreted" split
and your **no-name-bridge** (crossing into a physical register needs a comparison map + calibrated
apparatus + observational model + uncertainty budget + provenance; resemblance alone proves neither
numerical equality nor process equivalence) are the toll on that arrow — and not one cent of that toll
can be paid from inside the model.

## Precedent (in case you want to ground it)

The conclusion is not novel to us; it has a lineage that converges on it exactly:

- **Robert Rosen, the *modeling relation*** (*Anticipatory Systems*, 1985; *Life Itself*, 1991) — the
  exact skeleton. A formal system **F** models a natural system **N** only via two arrows: *encoding*
  ε: N→F (measurement, world read in) and *decoding* δ: F→N (prediction, pushed out), valid only when
  the diagram **commutes**. F alone models nothing. Your contravariant naming = F's internal inference;
  your covariant answer = ε, which requires an apparatus touching N; your two-pass = the commutativity.
- **Stevan Harnad, the Symbol Grounding Problem** (1990) + the **Total Turing Test (T3)** — a symbol
  system can only refer to more symbols ("Chinese from a Chinese–Chinese dictionary"); grounding needs
  sensorimotor capacity, i.e. robotic (T3), not merely linguistic (T2).
- **Rodney Brooks, "Intelligence Without Representation"** (1991) — *"the world is its own best model."*
  The field's own turn away from the disembodied model, toward the robot. That is your "the world must
  be allowed to answer."
- **Searle's Robot Reply** (to *Minds, Brains, and Programs*, 1980) — the *named* position that a body
  with sensors and actuators is what would ground the symbols.
- **Kant** (*Critique of Pure Reason*, 1781) — "Thoughts without content are empty, intuitions without
  concepts are blind." A discursive intellect cannot generate its own object; it must *receive* the
  given. (You already cite Kant in the preface — this is the line that bears on it.)
- **Percy Bridgman, operationalism** (*The Logic of Modern Physics*, 1927) — "the concept is synonymous
  with the corresponding set of operations." No operation, no concept. That *is* the no-name-bridge,
  stated 98 years early.
