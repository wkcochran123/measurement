# Volume 4 — Scientific Computation: An Out-of-Order History of Experimental Science

*Beastmaster spec, 2026-07-09, from the operator's structure. Vol 4 is the coffee-table / Hofstadter volume:
the whole device explained to a general reader, three ways, generously cited. The pilot is the α result
(four explanations of the fold probe); Vol 4 scales the same treatment to the whole code.*

## The one rule that governs everything: NO invented words
Vol 4 **never says "tange" or "funge."** Vols 1–3 define them (funge = *"this is just an indifferent thing
right now"* = the pooled, the world's, covariant; tange = *"this is a true thing right now"* = the selected,
ours, contravariant — the same four-number object `(spin, x, y, z)` and the same invariant, read two ways).
Vol 4 keeps the *lesson* and drops the *vocabulary*, carrying the distinction in **citation texture** instead:

- **Cited generously = funge.** Standard, shared, the world's. Credit every scientist and engineer whose idea
  touches the point — as generously as possible. The reader rests on known ground.
- **Uncited = tange.** Ours, novel — no name to cite because it hasn't existed before. **That silence is the
  signal to pay attention.**

So generosity is the *instrument*: bury the standard parts under citations, and the genuinely new parts become
legible **by contrast** — the text goes quiet exactly where the reader must wake up. The funge/tange split
becomes the *rhythm* of the prose, felt, not named. It is also honest: uncited = unproven-by-others, flagged
without a word of jargon.

## The four explanations (the deliverable form — a null basis by register)
Every unit of Vol 4 explains its piece of the code FOUR ways. Three are each stripped of two registers so they
share no vocabulary (maximal decorrelation); the fourth is the code itself:

1. **The mathematical explanation that uses no math** — the structure in pure intuition; no symbols, no equations.
2. **The physical explanation that uses no computation** — the physics as physics; no code, no algorithm.
3. **The computational explanation that uses no physics** — the code's logic as pure computation; no physical claims.
4. **A step-by-step walk-through of the code** — the literal Lean.

Three registers that can't borrow each other's words; their agreement + the code is the argument. This is the
Richardson null-basis as an expository form.

## Cite as many scientists and engineers as possible — generously
Maximal generosity is the design, not a courtesy. For every standard idea, name the people it came from and
connect the code's move to their experiment or theorem. The density is deliberate: it calibrates the reader's
attention (thick citation = coast; citation gap = the novel claim, pay attention). Reach across all of
experimental science and engineering — the out-of-order history is the whole point (structure over chronology).

## What migrates in / stays
- **The positron pun** ("The Doesn't-Matter Looks for Posit-Wrong Particles") — the tone-setter; the wordplay
  that turns out to *be* the structure (GEB move). Keep the playful register throughout.
- **The α derivation** is Vol 1's (with tange/funge). Vol 4 RE-explains the same result four ways, no invented
  words, generously cited — the pilot for the whole-code treatment.
