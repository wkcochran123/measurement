# 004 — Relative time from the four rulers, through the field equations

The author's four rulers: universe levels, heartbeats and time, combined to compute relative time through the
Einstein field equations. In the weak field a clock at potential Phi runs at 1 + Phi/c^2 (gravitational redshift),
and the potential is sourced by the energy density; on a line of universe levels the potential is a double sum of the
density, whose second difference is the density (receipt in Episode 12,
`the_second_difference_of_the_potential_is_the_density`).

- `gen_ladder.py K`, `probe_ladder.lean`: per level k = 1..K, the typeset ladder searched for rung k on a place one
  universe lower, on Episode 8's meter, with wall time: `LEVEL k raw blank ns`.
- `run_ladder.sh <CARD_2> <probe> <runs>`, `analyze_ladder.py <M2> <Ultra>`.
- `SEALED_PREDICTION.txt`: written before the full runs.
