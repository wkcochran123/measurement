## The cost of relabeling (why the boundary exists)
Renaming any name inside Episodes 1–15 triggers a **2+ day recompile** — the blast radius is total, because
nearly every file imports the base. **Relabeling is the most expensive operation in the project.** This is the
cost the whole α program has been about: the connection (the relabeling) *showing its price*, here in wall-clock
days. Treat every rename in the sacred slice as a two-day expenditure. Hoard them. Batch them. Never casual.

## The architecture (why the boundary is also correct, not just cheap)
Episodes 1–15 are the **flat funge base**: the invariant, the world's, the recession/rank, `cost = rank`, the
count that doesn't change. Territory 16+ is where the **tange** lives: the relabeling, the connection, the
precession, the fold, the holonomy — everything laid *over* the frozen base. **The 15/16 line is the funge/tange
line.** So the discipline is not a cage; it is the right shape: build the connection by adding NEW names and
wrappers in 16+ that relabel *without touching the sacred originals*. Read the sacred texts freely; write only
in 16+.
