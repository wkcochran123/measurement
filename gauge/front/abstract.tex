\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{chap:abstract}

A compiler's elaborator verifies that each new term is consistent with
accumulated context. A measurement records that each new event is consistent
with an accumulated ledger. This book argues these are the same operation and
traces the consequences: counting axioms yield a ledger; a ledger yields
admissibility; admissibility under consistent extension yields a continuum;
the continuum yields dynamics; dynamics yields physical law. The argument is
made concrete through a fifteen-episode Lean~4 formalization in which the
Lean elaborator itself serves as the instrument, and the final \lean{\#check}
serves as the experiment. No physical postulates are assumed. All structure
emerges from the grammar of verified distinction.
