# Measurement 137.7: The Tragedy of John Henry

Working storyboard for the Lean-as-manga cut-up. Each named Lean block is a
panel/cell. The code loosely holds the story together; the comments supply the
panel art, dialogue, genre collision, and misdirection.

## Overall Shape

| Range | Arc | Story function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-5 | Primer / The language gets invented | The reader, compiler, and narrator learn the first local grammar: fact, count, time, representation, source, execution. |
| 6-15 | The hammer gets made | Arithmetic, Galerkin, ideology, witness, locality, elaboration, measurement, tape, Atreyu, and inference are assembled. |
| 16-24 | Echo test / steel answer | The device transmits into the mountain, receives the echo, decodes the carrier, perceives recoil, records recoil, encodes the record, then continues into recovered observables and naming close. |
| 25-35 | The tunnel face | The single-character tape enters the corridor; rotation, ratios, alpha cells, slip bisection, charge/mass, orbit, Lorentz, Dirac, and grid tests cut into the mountain. |
| 36-40 | Tunnel test | The driven tunnel is stress-tested against recovered G, charge, second variation, and alpha. John can certify the route; he cannot get through globally. |

## The Single-Character Tape

The tape should be treated as one character, not a sentence. It is the smallest
possible glyph the house can keep adding rooms to.

| Recurring object | Story role | Lean anchors | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The tape cell | The single character John drives through the mountain. | `CompilerTape`, `CompilerTapeCell`, `CompilerOutput` | Keep it tiny. The growing story is not on the tape; it is around the tape. |
| The house that Jack built | A cumulative poem that keeps adding dependencies. | imports, typeclass ladders, `THEORY` rungs | Each stanza should add one dependency and repeat the prior chain. |
| The spike | One local obligation hammered into place. | each class/structure/theorem panel | Steel-driving rhythm: swing, receipt, swing, receipt. |
| The tunnel | The path of local witnesses. | `TRACED`, `EXECUTED_TRACE`, corridor/orbit/grid reports | The tunnel is tested, not escaped. |

### House Poem Seed

```text
This is the tape that John drove.
This is the fact that marked the tape that John drove.
This is the carrier that carried the fact that marked the tape that John drove.
This is the reader who doubted the carrier that carried the fact that marked the tape that John drove.
```

Every 1-3 episodes, add one stanza. The stanza should name exactly one new
dependency, then recurse until the line becomes funny, exhausting, and useful.
