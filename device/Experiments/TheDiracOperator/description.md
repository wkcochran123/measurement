## Description

This constraint is not a dynamical postulate but a restriction on
\emph{measurement}.  It determines the algebraic arena in which any
first--order propagation must exist.  In the $\Psi^{(4)}=0$ setting, the
tangent representation of refinement is necessarily linear,
and the informational degrees of freedom must transform under the
irreducible representations of the emergent Lorentz gauge
$g_{\mu\nu}$ developed in Section~7.4.

The Clifford algebra is therefore not an imposed structure; it is the
minimal bookkeeping device compatible with the metric gauge and with the
non--negativity of admissible refinement.  As shown in
Phenomenon~7.4.1, the spin representation appears when rotational
consistency is enforced on refinement counts.

\begin{equation}
  \gamma^{\mu}(\partial_{\mu}+iA_{\mu}) + m,
\end{equation}

informational square root of second--order propagation: the least
complexity operator whose iteration reproduces the spline extremal and
therefore respects the Master Constraint.

The Dirac equation is not an axiom of quantum mechanics in this framework.
It is the minimal and only admissible way to:
\begin{itemize}
\item preserve the rotational bookkeeping of measurement (spin),
\item transport informational components consistently with the metric gauge,
\item and maintain compatibility with the global condition $\Psi^{(4)}=0$,
  which bounds admissible curvature.
\end{itemize}
